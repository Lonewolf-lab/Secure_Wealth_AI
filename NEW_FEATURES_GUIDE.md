# 🚀 SecureWealth AI — New Features Summary Guide

This document provides a concise reference explaining what each of the three newly implemented financial intelligence features does within the **SecureWealth AI** platform.

---

## 1️⃣ Feature 1: Habitual Context & Recommendations

### 📌 Overview
The **Habitual Context Engine** analyzes the client's recurring financial behaviors, transaction patterns, spending cadence, and savings consistency to deliver personalized, explainable insights and automated micro-savings rules.

### ⚙️ Key Capabilities
* **Spending Cadence Analysis**: Identifies weekend spending spikes (e.g. +38% higher dining and cab expenses between Friday 8 PM and Sunday midnight vs. weekday averages).
* **Subscription Auditing**: Scans recurring monthly debits for overlapping or underutilized streaming services (e.g. flagging inactive subscriptions with zero interactions for 60+ days).
* **Salary Day Savings Trigger**: Detects uninvested cash sitting in liquid savings accounts after monthly salary deposits and prompts automated sweep transfers into high-yield liquid funds.
* **Explainability ("Why am I seeing this?")**: Every recommendation card provides a dedicated modal explaining the underlying data metrics and z-score trigger logic that generated the insight.
* **User Actions**: Allows clients to **Dismiss**, **Snooze for 30 days**, or **Activate Micro-Rules** (e.g., Round-up UPI purchases to nearest ₹50 into liquid FDs, or 24-hour impulse cooling-off locks).

## 2️⃣ Feature 2: Tax Savings Options Hub & Regime Solver

### 📌 Overview
The **Tax Savings Options Hub** transforms tax planning into an interactive, actionable dashboard that evaluates eligible tax-saving instruments and compares tax obligations under the Old vs. New Tax Regimes.

### ⚙️ Key Capabilities
* **Tax Instrument Options Catalogue**:
  * **PSB ELSS Wealth Tax Saver** (Section 80C, 3-yr lock-in, up to ₹46,800/yr tax saved).
  * **National Pension System NPS Tier-I** (Section 80CCD 1B, additional ₹50,000 deduction).
  * **Public Provident Fund PPF** (Section 80C, sovereign EEE tax-free interest).
  * **PSB Complete Health Shield** (Section 80D, health insurance deduction up to ₹75,000/yr).
* **Dynamic Old vs. New Regime Solver**: Interactive slider allowing users to adjust Gross Annual Income (₹6 Lakhs to ₹40 Lakhs) with instant side-by-side tax calculation and automated regime recommendation.
* **One-Click Investment Execution**: Interactive modal allowing users to directly allocate funds into their chosen tax-saving product while updating remaining Section 80C headroom in real time.

---

## 3️⃣ Feature 3: Active SIP Manager & Step-Up Scaling

### 📌 Overview
The **SIP Adjustment & Step-Up Manager** provides full control over active Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs), enabling dynamic contribution increases, annual salary step-up scheduling, and compounding wealth forecasts.

### ⚙️ Key Capabilities
* **Active SIP Controls**: Lists all active SIP commitments with real-time status toggles to **Pause** or **Resume** contributions on demand.
* **Annual Step-Up Scaling**: Interactive slider to schedule automatic annual percentage increases (+1% to +25% per year) aligned with annual salary hikes.
* **Compounding Wealth Multiplier**: Calculates 10-year compounding trajectories (assuming 12% p.a. CAGR), displaying:
  * **Total Capital Invested** over 10 years.
  * **Expected Corpus** at maturity.
  * **Pure Wealth Gain** generated by stepping up contributions annually.
* **Confirmation & Audit Timeline**: Side-by-side before-versus-after comparison modal confirming effective start dates and cash-flow impacts prior to submission.
